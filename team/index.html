The Collective

Filmmakers and AI specialists.

CineSmith is built by filmmakers who also build software. Across two decades we've directed and cut for Netflix, BBC, Channel 4, HBO, Discovery and others — while in parallel shipping cloud collaboration platforms, fintech and blockchain infrastructure, and creative AI products. CineSmith is what happens when the same hands hold both toolsets.

Founder
Filmmaker · Technologist · Founder

Taimur Khan

Filmmaker. 20+ years across documentary, drama, commercial, and broadcast — from the early 1990s Asian music video circuit to series editor seats on Johnny vs Amber (Discovery+) and The Russell Murders (Sky Documentaries). Editorial credits with Netflix, BBC, Channel 4, HBO, Discovery, Al Jazeera, Arte France, CNN, and The Economist. Recognition at Sundance and Cannes; films that have earned the Grierson. Founded WIDEI Films at Ealing Studios in 2004.

Technologist. Over a decade building software for the people who make screen work. In 2009 launched Skylight (skylightit.com), a cloud SaaS for project management, tasks, time billing and invoicing — covered by BBC News, The Guardian, and the FT. In 2017 joined Outlier Ventures, working on Money Circles, Buy.co and OpenData — fintech, public-sector procurement and big-data projects built on blockchain and ML/AI. Today, leads the AI automation, agentic workflow design, and prompt engineering work powering CineSmith. Computer pioneer since the ZX Spectrum / Commodore 64 days. Holds an active pilot's licence.

Entrepreneurial. A consistent track record of building companies across screen and software. WIDEI Films at Ealing Studios (2004) — production company; series for Sky and the Royal Film Commission of Jordan, alongside commercials and animation. Blueberry Creative — talent agency connecting TV and film editors, designers, and directors to broadcaster projects (BBC, Channel 4, CNN, HBO and others); scaled to £7m+ turnover. Skylightit.com (2009) — cloud SaaS for project management, tasks, time billing and invoicing; press coverage from BBC News, The Guardian, the FT. Skylight Interactive — sister company, digital operations and process automation built on AWS for clients including UK ports, AMEY, and Royal Mail. Creative Development Academy — emerging-filmmaker training delivered through the Documentary Filmmakers Group, Rose Bruford College, and the Royal Film Commission of Jordan. CineArt AI (2024) and CineSmith (current).

Now bringing both careers — pipeline and post knowledge from the cutting room and a working engineer's hands — to the agentic platform underneath CineSmith.

Dita Pesek

Filmmaker and technologist who works at the seam between cinematic craft and applied AI. Director, writer, and producer with short films at Cannes, and reality TV directing credits across the Czech Republic and Panama.

Crew credits include Oliver Twist (Polanski) and From Hell (Hughes Brothers). Studied directing and screenwriting at film school in the Czech Republic; financed her short Crossroads through one of the earliest crowdfunds led by a filmmaker.

Outside the cut, a sought-after speaker on cybersecurity, application security, and social engineering — including a featured talk at BSides London on hacking humans. Designs and builds the CineSmith product alongside Taimur.
