  • Waitlist details. When you join the CineSmith waitlist, we collect your email address and any further details you choose to give in the sign-up form (such as your name, company, and role).

2. How we use it

  • To manage the waitlist and contact you about beta access, launch news, and product updates for CineSmith (with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising. Every update email we send includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can also just reply asking to be removed.

We use a small number of service providers to run this site and store data on our behalf:

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, transfers are protected by recognised safeguards such as the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

4. How we protect it

Access to personal data is limited to the people who need it, protected by access controls and encryption in transit. Our providers hold recognised security certifications for the infrastructure they run for us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable, proportionate steps to protect your data and will inform you and the ICO of any breach where the law requires it.

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our infrastructure providers (such as Cloudflare) may set strictly necessary cookies for security and performance purposes only.

6. How long we keep it

We keep waitlist details until you ask to be removed or until the waitlist is closed and no longer needed. Email correspondence is kept for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with your enquiry and maintain our records.

7. Children

This site and the CineSmith beta are aimed at working professionals and are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16; if you believe a child has given us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

8. Automated decision-making

We do not use your personal data to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you, and we do not profile you.
